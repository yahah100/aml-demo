Exercise 1: Welding Sample Analysis

Dataset Description

The dataset consists of electrical measurements with two classes, showing an imbalanced distribution of 90,746 and 67,219 samples respectively. The data was collected across four different experiments, with varying sample sizes: 35,521, 17,913, 46,768, and 57,763 measurements. The voltage measurements range from 0.07V to 101.49V, with a mean of 22.04V (±6.02V standard deviation) and a median of 20.84V. The current measurements show a wider spread, ranging from -1.18A to 614.15A, with a mean of 173.46A (±180.84A standard deviation) and a median of 68.59A. The substantial difference between the mean and median current values, along with the large standard deviation, suggests a right-skewed distribution with some high-current outliers.
